Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the ModuloX Android application handles information.

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Developer: DR.IT.Studio

Contact: support@modulox.app

1. Summary

ModuloX does not collect or send personal information or project contents to DR.IT.Studio. The app does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The current version does not use a network connection.

2. Information you enter

When creating projects, you may enter project and room names, room dimensions, the number and dimensions of windows and doors, selected materials, and values used in calculations. This information is used to calculate construction material quantities and save your projects.

Project information is stored locally in the app database on your Android device. The database is not encrypted by the app. Project information is not transmitted to the developer.

3. Permissions and network access

The current version does not request Internet access and does not send entered information to servers. Calculations do not require a network connection.

4. Third-party services

The app does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. If you install the app through an app store, that store may process installation or device information under its own privacy policy.

5. Storage and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete a project in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. DR.IT.Studio does not hold a copy of your projects and cannot delete them remotely.

6. Support messages

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request; your email provider may also process the message. Contact: support@modulox.app.

7. Children

ModuloX is not directed to children under 13. We do not collect information about children through the app.

8. Policy changes

If the app or its data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Please review the policy when using a new version of the app.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer at support@modulox.app.

Note: This policy describes the current version of ModuloX. If a future version adds network features or third-party services, this policy will be updated to explain their data practices.
