1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FuleoMX ("the App"), developed by DR.IT.Studio, handles your data. FuleoMX is a fully offline utility app that includes a fuel calculator, scientific calculator, and unit converter.

FuleoMX does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline with no internet access.

2. Data Collection

FuleoMX does not collect any personal data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports sent to external servers
  • No cookies or tracking technologies
  • No advertising identifiers

3. Internet Access

FuleoMX does not request or use internet permission. The app cannot connect to the internet in any way. All functionality works completely offline.

4. Data Storage

All data created within the app is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Fuel records — saved in a local database (Room) on your device
  • Calculation history — saved in a local database on your device
  • App settings (theme, language, dark mode preference) — saved in local SharedPreferences on your device

This data is never transmitted, synced, or backed up to any external server. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data.

5. Third-Party Services

FuleoMX does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect data. There are:

  • No advertisements are displayed, and no advertising SDKs are included
  • No analytics services (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics)
  • No social media integrations
  • No third-party tracking tools

6. Permissions

FuleoMX does not request any device permissions. The app does not access:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or phone
  • Location
  • Storage (external)
  • Network or internet
  • Any other sensitive device features

7. Children's Privacy

FuleoMX does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages as it operates entirely offline and does not request any personal information.

8. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing the app data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

No data exists on external servers, so no additional deletion requests are necessary.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
