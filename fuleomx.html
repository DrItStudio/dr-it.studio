Key Features

Three powerful tools in one lightweight app

⛽

Fuel Calculator

4 calculation modes: consumption, distance, fuel needed, and trip cost. Save fuel records with statistics and track your average consumption over time.

🧮

Calculator

Full-featured calculator with calculation history. Save important results with custom labels for future reference.

📐

Unit Converter

10 categories: length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data size, pressure, and energy. Quick conversions at your fingertips.

🎨

7 Beautiful Themes

Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Lavender, Midnight, Rose, and Aurora. Choose light or dark mode, or follow your system preference.

🔒

100% Private & Offline

No internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. All data stays on your device. Zero data collection — your privacy is guaranteed.

🌍

Multilingual

Available in English and Ukrainian. Simple and clean interface designed for ease of use.

About FuleoMX

A lightweight, privacy-first utility for everyday calculations

100% Local Storage

All your data — fuel records, calculations, and settings — is stored only on your device. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

No Permissions Required

FuleoMX doesn't request any special permissions. No access to camera, contacts, location, or any other sensitive data.

Built with Modern Tech
